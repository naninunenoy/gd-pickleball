# Court practice rules

Play upright on a phone against two automatic opponents. Both drills share a fixed overhead court view. The former wall has been removed.

## Drills and teams

- **RALLY** is a one-bounce exchange. Both the opponent and the near receiver collect a ground bounce before returning a deep shot.
- **VOLLEY** exchanges unbounced balls at the kitchen line. At the baseline, let the opponent’s shot land inside the court and return it after one bounce. Short dinks also require a bounce.
- The left selector changes drill while idle, after a miss or while paused. The right selector chooses one or two near-side athletes; the opponents always have two. New games start with two near athletes.
- Each drill remembers its near-team size, line preference and personal best. Changing drill/team size restarts the exchange; changing line does not.

## Position button

- The large bottom button switches between **KITCHEN LINE** and **BASELINE** during play or while idle.
- Both near athletes move together in depth at the same speed. Their horizontal movement still follows their own lanes and the selected receiver.
- Pressing the button changes the formation only. It does not count as a hit, cancel a queued hit or reset the rally.
- At the kitchen, both may advance to collect a short bounced dink and recover to the chosen line after contact. Staying at the baseline can leave a short ball out of reach, so move forward in time.

## Shots

- Tap the court to start or return when lifting your finger. Long presses add no power.
- A short swipe dinks into the opposing kitchen; a long swipe plays deep. Left/right aims the course; long up/down input lifts or drives. The preview is blue for a dink and green for a deep stroke.
- Early input queues one shot until legal contact. Baseline returns, dinks and RALLY shots wait for the first bounce. One gesture returns one ball.
- In VOLLEY at the kitchen line, a normal tap on a low bounced ball keeps dinking. A baseline tap plays a deep stroke. A long swipe can send it deep. RALLY taps resume deep one-bounce shots.
- Volleys require feet outside the kitchen, including the line. Bounced balls may be hit inside it. A second near-side bounce ends the exchange.
- P1 owns the left half and P2 the right; the incoming course selects the receiver. Central balls go to the partner. No manual player-switch button is needed.
- Leaving the app or pausing cancels pending input. Tap the court to resume; a fresh gesture hits. Desktop click or Space / Enter uses the same gesture; P / Escape pauses and R resets.
