# Wall rally rules

One player, one practice wall. Play upright on a phone and try to beat your longest rally. All in-game text stays English.

## Tap to play

- Tap anywhere on the court or the large bottom pad to serve.
- The wall sends the ball back. Let it bounce once.
- When the pad says **TAP NOW**, tap to return the ball to the wall.
- Your player moves into position automatically. You only control swing timing.
- Each successful return adds one to your rally. The opening serve does not count.
- A second ground bounce ends the rally. Tap to start again; your best stays saved on this device.

There is one shot. No dragging, aiming, double-tapping, or Soft / Hard selection is needed. A tap slightly before the return window is buffered for 0.18 seconds; it does not arm an unlimited automatic return. Taps while the ball travels to the wall expire normally.

The ball gradually speeds up as the rally grows. The wall varies the return from side to side, within the player's automatic reach. Only returns after the first ground bounce are legal.

## Screen and pause

The game uses a portrait court view. On a wider screen it keeps the same tall play area with space at the sides. The large bottom pad is reachable with either thumb. The Pause button freezes play; tap Resume or the court to continue without swinging. Leaving the browser tab or app pauses an active rally.

Desktop fallback: click or Space / Enter to serve and return. P / Escape pauses. R starts a fresh rally. Best is retained when restarting and can be reset by clearing the game's local storage.
